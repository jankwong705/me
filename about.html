About me

Bio:

My name is Jan Kwong. I am pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at UC San Diego. I have a passion for software engineering and higher education research. While I was an Undergraduate Research Assistant at the UCSD LASR Lab, I participated in a project on automatic speech recognition models (ASR). I was also a computer science tutor at my school for the course of Theory of Computation.

In my research assistant role, I read and analyzed papers on different ASR models, such as Whisper, an ASR model by Open-AI, and implemented Python scripts that automate the computation of word error rate of the recognized speech output. Through this project, we've discovered that humans outperform AI models on isolated words, suggesting potential limitations in models’ training data or acoustic context requirements.

In my computer science tutor role, I held weekly office hours for students, explained course concepts patiently, and discussed concerns with the instructor. I also graded homework and exams from students, giving them insightful and useful feedback.

I enjoy working with students and researchers who share my interest in learning and innovation. I value diversity, collaboration, and feedback, and I believe that I can bring unique perspectives and experiences to the team.
